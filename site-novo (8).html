Massachusetts RMV guide

What you need to know

The path to a Massachusetts license depends on your age. Here is the short version, in your language.

  1. Learner's permit at 16Pass the RMV knowledge test. A parent or guardian signs the application.
  2. Driver's education30 hours of classroom, 12 hours behind the wheel with an instructor, 6 hours of observation, plus a 2-hour parent class.
  3. 40 hours of supervised drivingPractice with a licensed driver who is 21 or older.
  4. Road test at 16½ or olderAfter holding the permit for 6 months with no violations.
  5. Junior operator rulesNo driving 12:30–5 AM without a parent, no passengers under 18 (except family) for the first 6 months, and no phone use at all.
  1. Learner's permitPass the RMV knowledge test. Bring your ID and proof of Massachusetts residency.
  2. PracticeDrive with a licensed driver 21 or older (licensed at least 1 year) in the front seat. Lessons with an instructor make it faster and safer.
  3. No driver's ed requiredAdults don't need the driver's education course required for teens.
  4. Road testSchedule it with the RMV when you feel ready, then drive with an examiner.
  5. Class D licensePass the road test and you get your Massachusetts driver's license.
Permit test: 25 questions

You need 18 correct answers to pass. The test is offered in many languages, including Portuguese and Spanish.

Road test day

Bring your permit, a sponsor 21+ licensed at least 1 year, and a registered, inspected car. Or use our school car.

A license for all residents

Since July 2023, Massachusetts residents can apply for a standard license regardless of immigration status (Work and Family Mobility Act).

Hands-free law

Drivers can't hold a phone while driving. Drivers under 18 can't use a phone at all.

Summary for guidance only. Rules, documents and fees can change. Always confirm at mass.gov/rmv.

FAQ

Common questions

What should I bring to my lesson?

Your learner's permit, comfortable shoes and glasses or contacts if you use them. If you're preparing for the road test, bring the documents the RMV asks for.

How long does it take to be ready for the road test?

It depends on your experience. Some students need a few lessons, others more. After your first lesson, your instructor will tell you honestly how many lessons you'll likely need.

Can I use the school car for my road test?

Yes. We offer a car for test day, usually with a short warm-up lesson before your appointment. Call to check availability for your test date.

What documents does the RMV ask for?

Usually proof of identity, proof of Massachusetts residency and your Social Security number (or proof you're not eligible). Massachusetts also accepts certain foreign documents. The list changes, so always check the official checklist at mass.gov/rmv before your appointment.

How do I book my first lesson?

Call us, send a WhatsApp message or fill out the form below. Tell us your level, your language and the best times for you, and we'll confirm a day and time.

Do you teach in Portuguese and Spanish?

Yes. Tell us your preferred language when you call or book, and we'll match you with the right instructor.
